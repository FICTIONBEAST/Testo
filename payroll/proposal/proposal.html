Every daily entry in the notebook is consistent: start time, finish time and the written hours agree on all 22 shifts. The mistake is only in the adding-up at the bottom of the page.

The running total reaches exactly 109 hours on 17 October. The last two shifts, 18 Oct (5h) and 20 Oct (6h), were left out of the total.

How it works

  1. Photograph the notebook pages with a phone.
  2. AI reads each page twice, independently.
  3. Automatic checks compare the readings and test the arithmetic of every row.
  4. You get a spreadsheet and a PDF with the photo beside the numbers, ready to sign.

Why this is reliable when a chatbot alone is not

Asking a chat assistant to “read this page and add it up” gives one answer with nothing checking it. Handwriting gets misread and sums drift, and you cannot tell when. Here the AI only does the reading. The notebook then checks itself, because each row carries three numbers that must agree:

CheckWhat it catches
Two independent readings agreeA digit the AI misread
Finish minus start equals the written hoursA misread time, or a slip by whoever wrote it
Dates run in order, closed days are all MondaysA skipped row, or rows shifted by a day
Every shift is a sensible lengthUnreadable or impossible values
Notebook's grand total equals the sum of the daysAdding-up mistakes, like the 11 hours above

If any check fails, the record is marked NOT VERIFIED and names the row to look at. Six kinds of deliberately planted error were tested; all six were caught.
